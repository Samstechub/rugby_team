# Bernadette Olesia Rugby Foundation Website

A responsive website for the **Bernadette Olesia Rugby Foundation**, an organization dedicated to empowering girls through rugby, education, mentorship, and leadership development.

The website is designed around storytelling rather than aggressive fundraising. It introduces visitors to the girls, explains the foundation's mission, shows its programs and impact, and then provides an opportunity to support the girls' journey.

---

## 🌍 About the Foundation

The Bernadette Olesia Rugby Foundation empowers girls through rugby, education, and leadership development.

The foundation creates pathways for young women to pursue professional sport and sustainable livelihoods while upholding character, dignity, and fair play.

### Mission

> The Bernadette Olesia Rugby Foundation empowers girls through rugby, education, and leadership development. We create pathways for young women to pursue professional sport and sustainable livelihoods while upholding character, dignity, and fair play.

### Vision

> A world where girls everywhere can use sport as a tool to thrive — economically, socially, and personally — and to lead with integrity.

---

## 🎯 Website Goals

The website aims to:

- Introduce visitors to the foundation and its mission
- Tell Bernadette Olesia's story
- Highlight the girls and their journeys
- Explain the foundation's programs
- Demonstrate the organization's impact
- Build trust with supporters and partners
- Make donating simple and transparent
- Provide an easy way to contact the foundation

The primary design philosophy is:

**Story → Connection → Trust → Impact → Support**

The website should not feel like it is constantly asking visitors for money. Instead, visitors should first understand the mission and the people behind it before being invited to support the work.
