Our Story

She Believed There Was A Better Way.

Bernadette Olesia dedicated her life to rugby because she believed it could give her an honest living and a better future.

She refused to take shortcuts.

She believed success should never come at the cost of character, dignity or integrity.

The Bernadette Olesia Rugby Foundation exists to keep that belief alive.

Our Mission

Empowering Girls To Pursue Sport, Education And Sustainable Livelihoods.

We create pathways for young women to pursue professional sport and sustainable livelihoods while upholding character, dignity and fair play.

What We Do

Turning Potential Into Opportunity.

We create an environment where girls can train, learn, develop and prepare for the future.

Rugby Training

Free coaching, equipment and safe playing spaces help girls develop their sporting ability, confidence and discipline.

Education & Life Skills

Scholarships, financial literacy, health education and practical life skills help girls prepare for life beyond the field.

Mentorship

We connect girls with role models, mentors and career pathways in sport and beyond.

Integrity First

We teach girls that success means more when it is built on character, dignity, honesty and fair play.
